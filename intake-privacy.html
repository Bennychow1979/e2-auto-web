What this form does

Send information to E2 for a vehicle financing enquiry without creating an account. Email is optional. This form does not verify your identity, submit an application to a bank or guarantee approval.

Information you provide

The form collects your contact and identity details, addresses, employment or business information, financing preferences, emergency contacts and, where requested, guarantor details. You may attach IC, driving licence, income and bank or EPF statements. Only provide other people’s information with their permission.

Who can access it

After submission, your assigned salesperson and E2 Super Admin can review your details and documents. When they hand the enquiry to a selected Office Admin, that staff member also receives access to process it. Other staff do not receive access simply because they have an E2 account. Records and files use E2’s Supabase service in Singapore; files are stored in a private bucket.

Before and after submitting

Unsubmitted form answers and selected files stay in this open browser page. Reloading or closing it loses that progress. Clicking Submit starts an upload session lasting two hours; partial uploads can remain on the server if the connection fails. Abandoned uploads become eligible for cleanup 24 hours after that session expires. Cleanup runs on later invitation requests, so removal is not immediate.

A submitted enquiry stays with E2 for follow-up. Your invitation link cannot retrieve submitted answers or files. To correct information, provide missing documents or request deletion, contact your salesperson or e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com. This form does not subscribe you to marketing.
