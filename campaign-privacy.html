Visit measurement / Pengukuran lawatan / 访问统计

20 November 2026 · E2 AUTO SDN BHD

Your choice

For supported campaign links, we ask before measuring visits. If you allow it, E2 stores a random browser identifier, campaign source and post identifier, landing page path, time and consent version in our Supabase database. The browser remembers your choice and most recent campaign for up to 30 days. Visits to the same post within 30 minutes in the same browser are counted once.

If you register, an opaque visit reference connects your new account to that campaign. We count registration started and email verification completed separately. The most recent supported campaign within 30 days receives credit; a direct return does not replace it. This is approximate browser attribution, not identification of a unique person across devices.

Our measurement records do not copy your name, email, phone, password, full query string or application details. The registration reference is linked internally to the account ID. E2 administrators can view aggregate campaign results; anonymous visitors cannot read records. No registration event or account information is sent to Meta by this feature. Meta advertising cookies remain a separate choice.

You may reject or withdraw through “Visit measurement” on campaign/public account pages. Browsing and registration remain available. Withdrawal stops further collection and clears this browser's campaign reference; previous records are not erased automatically. Technical service logs may separately contain connection information. Data is used to compare E2 campaigns; contact us for privacy requests. Existing customer and advertising privacy notices remain applicable.

Pilihan anda

Dengan izin anda, E2 menyimpan ID pelayar rawak, sumber kempen, ID siaran, laluan halaman, masa dan versi persetujuan di Supabase. Pilihan dan kempen terkini disimpan dalam pelayar sehingga 30 hari. Lawatan siaran sama dalam 30 minit dikira sekali. Kempen terkini dalam 30 hari dikaitkan dengan akaun baharu melalui rujukan rawak. Pendaftaran bermula dan pengesahan e-mel selesai dikira berasingan.

Rekod pengukuran tidak menyalin nama, e-mel, telefon, kata laluan atau butiran permohonan. Rujukan akaun hanya dipautkan secara dalaman; pentadbir E2 melihat ringkasan. Ciri ini tidak menghantar pendaftaran kepada Meta. Tolak atau tarik balik melalui “Pengukuran lawatan”; carian dan pendaftaran tetap tersedia. Penarikan menghentikan pengumpulan seterusnya dan membuang rujukan pelayar, bukan rekod terdahulu. Kuki iklan ialah pilihan berasingan.

由你选择

只有你允许后，E2 才把随机浏览器编号、推广来源、帖文标识、落地页路径、时间及同意版本保存到 Supabase。浏览器最多保留选择和最近推广来源 30 天；同一浏览器在 30 分钟内重复进入同一帖文链接只计一次。注册时以随机引用关联最近 30 天内的推广来源，分别统计开始注册与完成邮箱验证。直接返回官网不会覆盖已有来源。

统计记录不复制姓名、电邮、电话、密码、完整网址参数或申请资料；注册引用只在内部关联账号编号，E2 管理员查看汇总。此功能不会向 Meta 发送注册事件或账号信息。可通过“访问统计设置”拒绝或撤回，浏览及注册仍可正常使用。撤回会停止之后的记录并清除浏览器来源，不自动删除过去记录。广告 Cookie 为另一项独立选择；跨设备及拒绝统计的访问无法完整归因。
