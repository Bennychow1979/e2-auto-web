Your loan application details.

This notice explains the E2 website workflow. A draft or submission to E2 is not a loan approval or an offer from a financier.

What you choose to provide

The application collects your selected vehicle and preferred deposit and tenure; name, identity number, contact details, addresses, household and employment information; certificate availability; self-reported credit concerns and outstanding amounts; emergency contacts; and guarantor information when needed. You may upload your own IC, driving licence and income documents. Worker checklists request three months of payslips, three months of personal bank statements and an EPF statement. Self-employed / business-owner checklists request six months of business / company bank statements for a vehicle purchased in your own name. A self-reported credit answer is not a verified credit report.

Why E2 uses it

E2 stores your draft, helps you complete the application and follows up on your financing enquiry. Only you fill in, change and confirm the applicant details. Sales staff can request corrections and record actual progress. The site does not automatically submit your information to a bank or make a credit decision. E2 will discuss the intended financier and any further documents or authorisations with you separately.

Who can see it

Before you submit, the draft is accessible through the application to you alone. After submission, E2 Super Admin and the active salesperson or Admin assigned to follow up can view it. Other salespeople, unassigned Admins, Account staff and public visitors cannot access the application. Super Admin can reassign a submitted application and this is recorded. Your application information does not appear on public car pages or the ordinary Customers directory.

Your supporting documents

Document files follow the same draft and submitted access rules above. Staff may view and download permitted files but cannot upload, replace or remove them for you. You can upload or replace files in Draft, Submitted to E2 or Needs information; later stages are read-only unless E2 requests more information. Replacement keeps the old file until the new upload succeeds. Removing a file hides it from staff immediately and requests storage deletion; if interrupted, cleanup retries when you reopen Documents. Copies already downloaded by authorised staff are not recalled. An uploaded file or month count is not proof of verification or approval.

Other people’s information

Tell emergency contacts and any guarantor how their details will be used and obtain their permission before including them. Provide only the requested information needed for this application. A guarantor's listing here does not replace their own agreement or a financier's requirements.

Storage and your choices

Application data is stored using E2's Supabase database and private file storage in Singapore. Files are accessed through a signed-in, authorised account; no public document link is published. Drafts are saved to your account rather than browser form storage. E2 retains information while needed for the enquiry, application and related records. For access, correction, withdrawal or deletion requests, contact e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com and quote your application reference; do not email your full IC or financial details unnecessarily. Some completed transaction records may need to be retained separately. No marketing subscription is created by applying.

Ringkasan Bahasa Melayu

Maklumat digunakan oleh E2 untuk menyimpan draf, melengkapkan permohonan dan membuat susulan pembiayaan. Draf hanya boleh dilihat oleh anda melalui aplikasi. Selepas dihantar, Super Admin E2 dan kakitangan aktif yang ditugaskan boleh melihatnya. Jurujual tidak boleh mengubah maklumat pemohon. Dapatkan kebenaran kontak kecemasan dan penjamin sebelum memberikan maklumat mereka. Dokumen IC, lesen memandu dan bukti pendapatan mengikut senarai semak anda disimpan secara peribadi melalui Supabase di Singapura. Akses dokumen mengikut peraturan draf dan permohonan dihantar yang sama. Kakitangan dibenarkan melihat dan memuat turun sahaja; pemohon sendiri memuat naik, mengganti atau membuang fail pada peringkat yang dibenarkan. Muat naik tidak bermaksud dokumen telah disahkan atau pinjaman diluluskan. Hubungi e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com untuk akses, pembetulan, penarikan atau permintaan pemadaman. Penghantaran kepada E2 bukan kelulusan pinjaman; penghantaran kepada pembiaya memerlukan susulan berasingan.
