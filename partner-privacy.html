When you submit a referred enquiry, E2 Auto saves your name, phone number, vehicle, referral source, viewing preference and the time you agreed to this notice. E2 uses these details to contact you, arrange viewings, track sales progress and administer referral commissions.

Your referrer can see an anonymous reference, sales stage and their commission. They cannot see your name, phone number, loan application or documents in the Partner portal. E2 staff access depends on their assigned role.

Referral links store a code in this browser for up to 30 days. Browsing alone does not create a customer lead. Visits are recorded separately and do not earn commission. The first identified enquiry for a phone number keeps its original referrer; later links do not change it.

Opening WhatsApp prepares a message in WhatsApp. No message is sent automatically. WhatsApp operates under its own terms. A submitted viewing preference is not a confirmed appointment.

For correction, deletion or questions about your information, contact sales@e2auto.my. E2 may need to retain transaction and commission records. Do not enter identity-document or banking details in this enquiry form.
