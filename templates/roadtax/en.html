    Common questions

    What is the difference between saloon and non-saloon?

    JPJ has two schedules for private cars. The class comes from the JPJ registration (check the geran or MyJPJ), not from the marketing label. For small engines the non-saloon rate is higher; for large engines it is lower.

    Where do I find the exact cc?

    On the geran (Vehicle Ownership Certificate / VOC) or in the MyJPJ app. A “1.5” is usually 1,496–1,498 cc and a “2.0” usually 1,997–1,999 cc — a few cc changes the amount for engines above 1,600 cc.

    How are hybrids taxed?

    Hybrids and plug-in hybrids are taxed by engine cc, the same as petrol cars. Only fully electric cars use the kW schedule.

    Why does a company-registered car cost more?

    In Peninsular Malaysia, a saloon registered to a company uses a higher schedule. For non-saloons, and in Sabah and Sarawak, the rate is the same.
