    Common questions

    How is the premium worked out?

    Comprehensive basic premium = the rate for the first RM1,000 (by cc) + RM26 for each further RM1,000 (Peninsular). Then the NCD comes off, and 8% SST plus RM10 stamp duty are added.

    Why does an insurer quote differently?

    Motor tariffs were liberalised from 1 July 2017. Insurers may use other factors such as driver age, claims record and car model, so the price can be higher or lower.

    What is NCD?

    The no claim discount. Each claim-free year raises it, from 25% up to a maximum of 55%. It belongs to the driver, not the car, and can be transferred when you change cars.

    Do I need comprehensive cover with a loan?

    Yes. Banks and credit companies usually require comprehensive insurance for a financed car.
