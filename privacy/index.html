2. What we collect

We collect personal data that you give us directly when you enquire about a vehicle or apply for vehicle financing through us:

  • Identity data — name, NRIC/passport number, date of birth
  • Contact data — phone number, email address, home or mailing address
  • Employment and income data — employer, occupation, salary slips, EPF/KWSP statements, bank statements
  • Credit-related data — information relating to your existing loan commitments and credit standing, where required to assess a financing application
  • Vehicle interest data — the models you enquire about, trade-in vehicle details
  • Communication data — messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or by email

We also receive limited data automatically when you interact with our Facebook or Instagram pages and advertisements (for example, aggregated statistics on post reach and engagement provided by Meta).

3. Why we use it

We use your personal data only for these purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and advise you on available vehicles
  • To prepare and submit vehicle financing or credit applications to banks and licensed financial institutions on your behalf
  • To arrange trade-in valuation, insurance, vehicle registration and delivery
  • To keep records required by law, including under vehicle registration and anti-money-laundering rules
  • To contact you about your application status or about vehicles matching your stated requirements

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

To provide the service you ask us for, we may disclose your data to:

  • Banks and licensed financial institutions, for the purpose of applying for vehicle financing or credit facilities on your behalf
  • Insurance companies and agents, where you ask us to arrange insurance
  • The Road Transport Department (JPJ) and other government authorities, where required for registration or by law
  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, IT and messaging platforms), bound to keep your data confidential

We disclose only what is necessary for the purpose, and only with your consent or where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as needed to complete your enquiry or financing application, and afterwards only for as long as required by Malaysian law or to resolve disputes. Data no longer needed is securely destroyed.

6. Your rights under the PDPA

You may at any time:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Withdraw consent to further processing, or ask us to stop using your data for marketing
  • Limit how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com or WhatsApp +60 12-278 5126. We will respond within 21 days as required by the PDPA.

Please note that if you withdraw consent, we may no longer be able to process a financing application on your behalf.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Documents you send us (such as salary slips or bank statements) are used only for your application and are not shared beyond the parties listed in Section 4.

8. Children

Our services are for persons aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this page, with the "Last updated" date shown above.
