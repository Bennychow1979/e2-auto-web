Roles and workflow
Runner:
- Uses the existing staff sign-in. New Runner role is managed by Super Admin.
- Sees only currently assigned jobs and their own petrol claims on those jobs.
- Uploads PUSPAKOM appointment, job sheet and handover evidence.
- PUSPAKOM/ordinary trips: Assigned -> Car out -> Arrived -> Returning -> Car in.
- Carsome pickup: Assigned -> Collected -> Car in.
- Petrol: save draft, upload receipt, submit to Admin.
- Cannot browse unpublished vehicle stock, repair bills, inspections, customer
  applications, another Runner's jobs, approvals or user permissions.

Inspector / Admin:
- Upload repair invoices against an existing vehicle inspection.
- The inspection editor links directly to that vehicle's repair bills.
- Admin assigns/reassigns unstarted jobs, can cancel with a reason, and
  approves or returns submitted petrol/repair expenses.

Account:
- Sees only approved, verified and paid expenses and the necessary job records.
- Reviews evidence, verifies an expense, uploads payment proof, then records
  actual payment date/reference.
- Can return a query to Admin; this removes the item from Account's queue until
  reapproved. New payment evidence is required after re-verification.
- Cannot change a submitted amount or approve an expense.
- Recording Paid does not execute a bank transfer.

Archive and concurrency
- Private operations-files bucket; no public file URLs.
- JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF; 10 MB per file; 50 files per job/expense including
  pending reservations. File picker and mobile camera input are provided.
- Archived files cannot be replaced/deleted by ordinary staff.
- Submitted amounts lock until Admin returns the expense.
- Revision checks reject stale edits and repeated payments.
- File reservation/upload/finalization verifies stored byte size and MIME.
- Submit, approval, return, verification, payment and movement events persist.
- Closed jobs retain documents but do not accept new job attachments.
- Reassigning an unstarted job immediately removes the former Runner's access,
  including their petrol claim on that job; Admin retains the full record.
- Movement records do not change a vehicle's sale/publication status.
