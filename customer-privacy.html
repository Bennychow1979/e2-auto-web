YOUR INFORMATION

Customer privacy notice.

Version: 13 September 2026 · E2 AUTO SDN BHD

What you share and why

We collect your name, email and mobile / WhatsApp number to create and manage your customer account and assist with your vehicle enquiries. You may also choose to provide your city, state, language, budget, preferred vehicle, purchase timing, payment preference and trade-in details. These extra fields are optional. You can browse vehicles without an account.

Who can access it

You can view and edit your profile. Active E2 Admin and Super Admin accounts can view customer profiles to assist with enquiries. Profiles are not displayed publicly, and Salesman accounts do not currently have access to this customer directory. Account information is stored using Supabase; this project's database is in Singapore. Confirmation and password-reset emails are sent using E2's configured email service. E2 staff cannot view your account password.

Your saved cars

When you choose Save car, we store the vehicle reference and date in your account so you can access your shortlist on other devices. Your saved-car list is visible only to your account through the website; it is not included in the staff customer directory. Remove a saved car at any time. A saved car does not reserve the vehicle.

Your choices

Creating an account does not subscribe you to promotional broadcasts. Your preferences do not constitute a financing application. Do not enter identity card numbers, banking credentials, income documents or other sensitive documents in these fields.

You can update or clear optional details in My E2. For help accessing or correcting your information, withdrawing your consent, closing your account or requesting deletion, contact e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you own the account before acting on a request. Account information is retained while needed to operate your account and handle your enquiries; retention required for separate transactions may differ.

Notis privasi pelanggan

E2 AUTO SDN BHD mengumpul nama, e-mel dan nombor telefon / WhatsApp anda untuk mengurus akaun pelanggan dan membantu pertanyaan kenderaan. Maklumat bandar, negeri, bahasa, bajet, kenderaan pilihan, masa pembelian, pilihan pembayaran dan tukar beli adalah pilihan. Anda boleh melihat kenderaan tanpa akaun.

Anda boleh melihat dan mengemas kini profil sendiri. Admin dan Super Admin E2 yang aktif boleh melihat profil pelanggan untuk membantu pertanyaan. Profil tidak dipaparkan kepada umum dan akaun Salesman tidak mempunyai akses kepada direktori pelanggan ini. Maklumat akaun disimpan menggunakan Supabase dengan pangkalan data projek ini di Singapura. E-mel pengesahan dan penetapan semula kata laluan dihantar melalui perkhidmatan e-mel E2. Kakitangan tidak boleh melihat kata laluan anda.

Apabila anda memilih Save car, rujukan kenderaan dan tarikh disimpan dalam akaun supaya senarai boleh diakses pada peranti lain. Senarai ini hanya dipaparkan kepada akaun anda, bukan dalam direktori pelanggan kakitangan. Anda boleh membuang kereta tersimpan pada bila-bila masa. Menyimpan kereta bukan tempahan kenderaan.

Pendaftaran tidak melanggan anda kepada siaran promosi. Pilihan pembiayaan bukan permohonan pinjaman. Jangan masukkan nombor kad pengenalan, kelayakan perbankan, dokumen pendapatan atau dokumen sensitif lain. Anda boleh mengubah atau memadam maklumat pilihan dalam My E2.

Untuk mengakses atau membetulkan maklumat, menarik balik persetujuan, menutup akaun atau meminta pemadaman, hubungi e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com. Pengesahan pemilikan akaun mungkin diperlukan. Maklumat disimpan selama diperlukan untuk mengurus akaun dan pertanyaan; tempoh penyimpanan bagi transaksi berasingan mungkin berbeza.
