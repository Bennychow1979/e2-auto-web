Advertising privacy.

14 September 2026 · E2 AUTO SDN BHD

Optional advertising measurement

If you allow advertising cookies, we load Meta Pixel (1050984971227179) on our homepage, showroom and published vehicle detail pages. It helps us measure advertising and show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram. Rejecting advertising cookies does not prevent browsing, WhatsApp enquiries, registration or loan applications.

What is shared with Meta

Events include page views, viewing a published vehicle, clicking a WhatsApp link and using the vehicle's monthly calculator. Vehicle events contain the public stock reference, brand / model and advertised price in MYR. A calculator event records use only; we do not send your chosen deposit, term, rate or monthly estimate. A WhatsApp event records a click, not a sent message or confirmed appointment. We do not include the WhatsApp message or destination phone number in event parameters.

Meta Pixel can also receive the page address, referring page address, browser / device information, IP address and advertising identifiers such as cookies or Facebook click identifiers. Meta may associate events with your Meta account for measurement and advertising. We disable automatic event configuration and do not supply customer information for advanced matching. See Meta's privacy policy for its processing and controls.

Pages excluded

We do not install this tracking on account, sign-in, customer profile, loan application, guest intake, document upload or staff pages. Draft vehicle previews are excluded. We do not send application answers, identity documents, bank statements, passwords, customer names, email addresses or customer phone numbers as advertising event data.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer of the homepage, showroom or a published car page. Choose Reject advertising to stop subsequent tracking and remove Meta's advertising cookies that this website can remove. Withdrawing does not erase data already received by Meta. Browser or Meta account controls may offer additional choices.

We store your choice in this browser for up to 180 days, then ask again. This preference is separate from sign-in and other necessary site storage. If browser storage is blocked, the choice may not persist across pages. Advertising blockers may prevent measurement even after consent.

Contact E2

Contact e2autosdnbhd@gmail.com about your privacy choices. Our customer privacy notice and loan privacy notice explain those separate services.

Pilihan kuki pengiklanan

Meta Pixel hanya dimuatkan selepas anda memilih Allow advertising, pada halaman utama, showroom dan butiran kenderaan yang diterbitkan. Anda boleh memilih Reject advertising dan masih menggunakan laman web, menghubungi E2 atau membuat permohonan.

Meta menerima peristiwa pelayaran, kenderaan yang dilihat, klik WhatsApp dan penggunaan kalkulator, serta maklumat sambungan / peranti dan pengecam pengiklanan. Nilai deposit dan anggaran bulanan anda tidak dihantar sebagai data peristiwa. Klik WhatsApp bukan pengesahan mesej dihantar atau janji temu. Halaman akaun, permohonan, muat naik dokumen dan kakitangan tidak dipasang penjejakan ini.

Ubah pilihan melalui Cookie settings di bahagian bawah halaman awam. Pilihan disimpan sehingga 180 hari dalam pelayar ini. Penarikan persetujuan menghentikan penjejakan seterusnya tetapi tidak memadam data yang telah diterima Meta.
